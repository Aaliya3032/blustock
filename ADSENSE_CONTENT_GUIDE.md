# AdSense Content Quality Improvement Guide

## 🔴 Current Issue: "Low Value Content" Policy Violation

Google AdSense has flagged your site for not meeting their content quality standards. This guide will help you fix this issue.

## 📋 What AdSense Considers "Low Value Content"

1. **Thin Content**: Pages with very little text (less than 300-500 words)
2. **Duplicate Content**: Content copied from other sites
3. **Auto-Generated Content**: Content created by scripts or tools
4. **Lack of Originality**: Not enough unique, valuable information
5. **Poor User Experience**: Hard to navigate, slow loading, broken links
6. **Insufficient Pages**: Not enough substantial content pages

## ✅ What Your Site Needs

### Minimum Requirements:
- **At least 10-15 pages** with substantial content (500+ words each)
- **Unique, original content** written specifically for your audience
- **Regular updates** showing the site is actively maintained
- **Clear navigation** and good user experience
- **About page** with detailed information about your business
- **Contact information** clearly visible
- **Privacy Policy** and **Terms of Service** pages

## 📝 Content Writing Guidelines

### DO's ✅
- Write **original, unique content** in your own words
- Aim for **500+ words per page**
- Use **proper headings** (H1, H2, H3) for structure
- Include **relevant keywords** naturally
- Add **images with alt text** to break up text
- Write for **your audience**, not search engines
- Update content **regularly** (at least monthly)

### DON'Ts ❌
- Don't copy content from other websites
- Don't use auto-generated or spun content
- Don't stuff keywords unnaturally
- Don't create pages with just a few sentences
- Don't use duplicate content across pages
- Don't create doorway pages (pages just for ads)
